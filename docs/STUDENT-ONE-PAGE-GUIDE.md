# ACE Canteen (CollegeBits) — 1-Page Student Quick Guide

**Order Link:** `https://<your-canteen-domain>/customer/` · **Live Token Board:** `/display` · **Policies:** `/legal`

---

## 1. Install App (Free · No App Store Needed)
* **iPhone (Safari, iOS 16.4+):** Open `/customer/` in **Safari** -> tap **Share (⎋)** -> **Add to Home Screen** -> **Add**. Launch **CollegeBits** from your Home Screen icon.
* **Android (Chrome):** Open `/customer/` in **Chrome** -> tap **Install app** (or **⋮ Menu -> Install app**).

---

## 2. Sign In, Set Allergens & Enable Order Alerts
1. Register with your **name**, **email**, and a **12+ character password** (or use quick guest OTP where enabled).
2. In **Account -> Profile, Dietary & Security**, save dietary tags or allergens (e.g., `peanuts, dairy`).
3. Tap **Enable push alerts** -> **Allow** so your phone alerts you immediately when the kitchen **accepts** your order and when it is **ready for pickup**. *(On iPhone, push works after installing to Home Screen.)*

---

## 3. Browse, Checkout & Payment Rules
1. Pick your **Counter** and **Pickup Break Slot**, add items (watch the cart badge), or tap **Ask AI Menu Concierge** for suggestions within your budget/allergens.
2. Complete payment via **UPI / Razorpay** or **Prepaid Campus Wallet**.
3. **Strict No-Refund Policy:** All confirmed payments and wallet checkouts are **final**.
   - You may cancel only **unpaid** pending orders before payment completes.
   - If the kitchen cannot prepare a paid order, your payment is preserved and flagged for counter resolution (substitute meal / admin handling). See [Terms & Privacy](/legal).

---

## 4. Collect Food Using Your Single-Use QR Pass
1. Wait for **"Order ready for pickup!"** notification (or check **My Orders** / the canteen **TV Display Board**).
2. Under your order, tap **Show Short-Lived Pickup QR** (or **Show large bright QR** and raise phone screen brightness).
3. Show the QR (or 6-character manual backup code) at your assigned counter. Each QR works **once** and expires automatically after the pickup window — never share it with others.

---

## 5. Offline Note
If campus Wi-Fi or mobile data drops, CollegeBits shows a clear **Offline** notice so you do not submit duplicate orders or stale payments. Reconnect and tap **Retry connection** to continue.
