ACE CANTEEN · COLLEGEBITS CAMPUS POLICY

Student Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to the CollegeBits Customer PWA (/customer/) and Mobile Application.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Strict No-Refund Policy

1. Student Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

  • Account details: Full name, college/personal email address, password hash (salted PBKDF2/Argon2 — plaintext passwords are never stored), optional phone number, dietary preference, and allergen tags.
  • Order & wallet activity: Items ordered, pickup slots, counter assignment, digital receipts, loyalty points, prepaid wallet balance movements, and pickup collection verification timestamps.
  • Optional notification tokens: When you tap Enable push alerts, we store your browser Web Push subscription endpoint and encryption keys (push_subscriptions) strictly to send order status updates.

What We Never Store or Share

  • Zero bank/PIN storage: Card numbers, UPI PINs, and bank credentials are processed exclusively by Razorpay over TLS — never stored on CollegeBits servers.
  • Privacy-preserving pickup & TV screens: Public queue boards (/display) and camera pickup URLs (/pickup) display only order/token numbers and preparation status — never student names, emails, or phone numbers.
  • Grounded AI privacy: Menu search and concierge recommendations do not send your name, email, phone number, or payment data to external AI APIs.
  • Zero third-party advertising: Student data is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Data Retention & Student Controls

  • Temporary operational artifacts (expired login sessions, expired pickup QR tokens, read non-financial alerts, diagnostic logs) are pruned after 4 days (TEMP_RETENTION_DAYS=4). Accounting ledgers, orders, and payment audit logs are retained under campus financial audit rules with encrypted off-host backups.
  • You can update dietary/allergen details or turn off Web Push notifications anytime from the Account section, or contact the canteen office for account deactivation.

2. Terms of Service

  • Campus accounts & security: Keep your password private (minimum 12 characters). Repeated failed sign-in attempts trigger temporary account/IP rate limiting.
  • Order reservation & pricing: Unpaid orders hold item stock briefly during checkout before expiring automatically if payment is not completed. Menu prices and GST are verified server-side.
  • Pickup verification: Once your order reaches Ready, present your short-lived pickup QR code or 6-character manual code at your assigned counter before expiry. Pickup tokens are single-use; after staff scans and confirms handover (Collected), the token cannot be reused.
  • Misuse: Sharing forged screenshots, attempting unauthorized API access, or harassing counter staff may result in account suspension under college rules.

3. Strict No-Refund Policy

  • All confirmed payments are final: Completed Razorpay UPI orders and prepaid campus wallet checkouts are strictly non-refundable. There are no automated or self-service cash/bank refund workflows in the application.
  • Before payment confirmation: Only unpaid pending orders may be cancelled prior to payment capture (restoring reserved loyalty points and item stock immediately).
  • Kitchen unavailability or late payment capture: If a paid order cannot be fulfilled due to a kitchen stockout/exception (kitchen_unfulfilled) or a delayed Razorpay capture after checkout expiry (late_capture_flagged), the payment record is fully preserved in the ledger and flagged as an administrative incident for counter resolution (such as an approved substitute item or canteen administration reconciliation).
